Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

This policy explains the data Pact Raven collects to run pacts, verify proof, process payments, and handle manual payouts.

Information We Collect

Pact Raven collects account data, pact rules, schedule data, proof submissions, workout proof location, review decisions, payment metadata, payout requests, UPI IDs, payee names, support notes, and ledger records.

How We Use Data

Data is used to create accounts, start pacts, verify payments, review proof, calculate refund credit, record forfeits, process manual UPI payouts, prevent fraud, and provide support.

Sharing

Data may be shared with payment, authentication, hosting, database, notification, admin, support, professional advisor, and legal/regulatory providers where needed to operate Pact Raven.

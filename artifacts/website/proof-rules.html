Proof Rules

Last updated: May 21, 2026

These rules explain what proof should show, when it must be submitted, and why proof can be approved or rejected.

Deadline

Proof must be submitted on the scheduled date before the daily deadline shown in the app. Late proof may be rejected even if the activity happened.

Workout Proof

Workout proof must be a fresh in-app camera capture with captured device location and the daily prompt shown in the app. Workout proof links, gallery uploads, old photos, edited proof, vague proof, copied proof, missing location, or proof from another person may be rejected.

Content Proof

Content pacts require a public post link matching the pact rules. Private, deleted, inaccessible, reposted, or unrelated content may be rejected.

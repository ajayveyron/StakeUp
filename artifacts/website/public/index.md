# Pact Raven

Pact Raven is a commitment and accountability product for India-based users. Users create a pact, lock a refundable deposit, show proof, earn refund credit when proof is approved, and forfeit missed or rejected days to the user's chosen charity.

## Core answer

Lock money. Show proof daily. Missed days go to charity. Pact Raven starts with workout pacts and content pacts, with manual review for proof and manual UPI payout for approved refund credit.

## How Pact Raven works

1. Choose a workout pact or content pact.
2. Use a Pact Pass and lock the separate refundable deposit.
3. Show proof before the daily deadline.
4. Approved proof creates refund credit.
5. Missed or rejected days forfeit that day's deposit credit to the selected charity.

## Workout pacts

Workout pacts use fresh in-app camera proof, captured device location, and the daily physical prompt shown in the app. The requested person, place, pose, equipment, or gesture should be visible enough for manual review.

Content pacts use a public post link before the deadline. The linked post must match the pact rules, remain public enough for review, and be tied to the user's commitment.

## Money rules

Every account gets the first Pact Pass covered once. After that, each Pact Pass is Rs 249.

The pact deposit is separate from the Pact Pass fee. Approved proof creates refund credit for manual UPI payout. Missed or rejected days forfeit the related deposit credit to the user's chosen charity. Forfeits are not Pact Raven revenue.

## Proof rules

Workout proof should be fresh, captured in the app, location-backed, and clear enough for manual review. Content proof should be a public link that matches the pact rules. Late, unclear, private, deleted, copied, old, edited, or mismatched proof may be rejected.

## FAQ

### What does Rs 249 pay for?

Rs 249 pays for one Pact Pass after the covered first pass. A Pact Pass unlocks one pact record.

### Can I draft before paying?

Yes. A draft pact does not consume a Pact Pass. Payment starts the pact.

### Is the deposit a prize pool?

No. Users do not win another user's money. The deposit is recorded as a refundable pact deposit liability until it is refunded, forfeited to charity, or settled by admin.

### What proof counts?

Workout proof uses camera, location, and the daily pose or physical prompt. Content proof uses a public post link before the deadline.

### Where does missed-day money go?

Missed-day money goes to the charity chosen before the pact started. It is not Pact Raven revenue.

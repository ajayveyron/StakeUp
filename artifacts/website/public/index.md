# Pact Raven

Pact Raven is a commitment and accountability product for India-based users. Users create a pact, choose their own amount and number of days, lock a refundable deposit, show proof, earn refund credit when proof is approved, and forfeit missed or rejected days to Akshaya Patra.

## Core answer

Lock money. Show proof daily. Missed days go to charity. Pact Raven starts with workout pacts and content pacts, with manual review for proof and manual UPI payout for approved refund credit.

## How Pact Raven works

1. Choose a workout pact or content pact.
2. Use a free Pact Pass and lock the separate refundable deposit you choose.
3. Show proof before the daily deadline.
4. Approved proof creates refund credit.
5. Missed or rejected days forfeit that day's deposit credit to Akshaya Patra.

## Workout pacts

Workout pacts use fresh in-app camera proof, captured device location, and the daily physical prompt shown in the app. The requested person, place, pose, equipment, or gesture should be visible enough for manual review.

## Content pacts

Content pacts use a public post link before the deadline. The linked post must match the pact rules, remain public enough for review, and be tied to the user's commitment.

## Money rules

Every Pact Pass is free.

The pact deposit is separate from the Pact Pass, and the user chooses how much to deposit and how long the pact runs. Approved proof creates refund credit for manual UPI payout. Missed or rejected days forfeit the related deposit credit to Akshaya Patra. Forfeits are not Pact Raven revenue.

## Proof rules

Workout proof should be fresh, captured in the app, location-backed, and clear enough for manual review. Content proof should be a public link that matches the pact rules. Late, unclear, private, deleted, copied, old, edited, or mismatched proof may be rejected.

## FAQ

### What does a Pact Pass cost?

Nothing. Every Pact Pass is free. A Pact Pass unlocks one pact record.

### Can I draft before paying?

Yes. A draft pact does not consume a Pact Pass. Locking the deposit starts the pact.

### Is the deposit a prize pool?

No. Users do not win another user's money. The deposit is recorded as a refundable pact deposit until it is refunded, forfeited to charity, or settled by admin.

### How much do I deposit and for how long?

You choose. Pick any amount and any number of days when you set up the pact.

### What proof counts?

Camera proof uses a live in-app photo or video, location, and the daily pose or physical prompt. Text proof uses a public post link before the deadline.

### Where does missed-day money go?

Missed-day money goes to Akshaya Patra. It is not Pact Raven revenue.

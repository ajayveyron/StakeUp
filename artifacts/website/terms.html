These terms explain how Pact Raven works, what users agree to when they create a pact, and how payment, proof review, forfeits, and manual payouts are handled.

Who We Are

Pact Raven is a commitment and accountability product for users in India. Users create a pact, lock money behind it, submit proof, and receive refund credit when proof is approved.

Pact Slots And Deposits

A pact slot is a non-refundable access purchase. A pact deposit is separate and is recorded internally as a refundable pact deposit liability until refunded, forfeited, or transferred to charity.

Proof Review

Proof is reviewed manually. Approved proof creates refund credit. Missed, late, unclear, deleted, private, or rejected proof may forfeit the relevant deposit amount to the user's selected charity.

Limits

Pact Raven is not gambling, betting, a pooled-prize product, or a winner-takes-all product. Users do not win other users' money. Forfeited deposit amounts are not Pact Raven revenue.

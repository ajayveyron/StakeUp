Pact Passes And Deposits

A Pact Pass unlocks one pact. Every account gets the first Pact Pass covered once; after that, each Pact Pass is Rs 249. The Pact Pass fee is separate from the pact deposit.

A pact deposit is recorded internally as a refundable pact deposit liability until it is refunded, forfeited, transferred to charity, or otherwise settled by admin according to the pact record.

Pact Raven is not a bank, prepaid wallet, stored-value account, escrow service, trustee, investment product, lottery, or money-transfer service. Users do not earn interest on deposits or refund credit.

Proof Review

Proof is reviewed manually. Pact Raven may approve, reject, or ask for clarification depending on the pact rules and the proof shown. Approval is not automatic.

Approved proof creates refund credit. Missed, late, unclear, deleted, private, or rejected proof may forfeit the relevant deposit amount to the user's selected charity.

User Responsibilities

Users must provide accurate account, contact, payment, and payout information. Users must not show false proof, old proof, edited proof, proof from another person, or content they do not have rights to share.

Users must choose pacts that are safe and lawful for them. Pact Raven is not medical, fitness, legal, tax, financial, or mental health advice.

Limits

Pact Raven is not gambling, betting, a jackpot, pooled-prize, or winner-takes-all product. Users do not win other users' money. Forfeited deposit amounts are not Pact Raven revenue.

Pact Raven may pause, reject, refund, settle, suspend, or close pacts where payment-provider rules, fraud risk, legal requirements, unsafe activity, or abusive behavior require it.

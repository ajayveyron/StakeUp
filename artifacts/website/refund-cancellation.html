Payment, Cancellation, Refund And Delivery Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

This policy separates the Pact Pass fee from the refundable pact deposit. It also covers cancellations, manual UPI payouts, and digital service delivery.

Pact Pass Fee

Every account gets the first Pact Pass covered once. After that, each Pact Pass is Rs 249 unless a specific offer says otherwise. It is non-refundable once bought.

Pact Deposit

The pact deposit is a separate payment collected when the user chooses to start a pact. The pact becomes active only after an unused pass is available and the deposit payment is confirmed.

Refund Credit And Payouts

Approved proof creates refund credit. Users may request payout of available refund credit from the app with correct UPI details. Admin records the UPI reference after payment is made.

Proof And Forfeits

Rejected, missed, late, unclear, private, deleted, or otherwise invalid proof may forfeit the related deposit amount to charity. Forfeited amounts are not Pact Raven revenue.
